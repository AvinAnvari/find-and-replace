How to Use Find and Replace

1
Set up your placeholders
In your spreadsheet, create two columns:
  • Column A: Placeholder text (what to find)
  • Column B: Replacement value (what to replace it with)
[[NAME]] John Smith
[[DATE]] January 19, 2025
[[COMPANY]] Acme Corp
2
Select your data
Highlight all the cells containing your placeholder-value pairs (both columns).
3
Name your output folder (optional)
Enter a name for the folder where edited copies will be saved. Leave blank to create the folder inside your selected source folder.
4
Select your template folder
Click "Select Folder" and choose the Google Drive folder containing your template documents. These are the Google Docs with placeholder text.
5
Get your edited documents
The add-on will create copies of each document with all placeholders replaced. You'll get a link to the new folder when complete.
💡 Tip
Your original template documents are never modified. The add-on only works on copies.
